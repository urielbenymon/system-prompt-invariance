# Human Grading Instructions and Participant Consent

## Participation and Consent

You are being asked to voluntarily assist with the human-validation component of an independent research project studying the evaluation of large language model (LLM) responses. Participation involves grading 54 model responses according to the instructions and scoring rubrics provided below.

Participation is **voluntary and unpaid**. No compensation is provided or expected for completing this rating task. By agreeing to participate, you acknowledge that you understand the nature of the task and that your ratings may be aggregated, analyzed, and reported as part of this research and related research materials.

No reasonably foreseeable risks beyond those associated with ordinary computer use and the time and effort required to complete the rating task are anticipated.

Personally identifying information about raters will not be reported in the research paper. Rater identifiers are used only as needed to organize and verify the human-validation data.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline to participate before beginning the task. If you begin the task, you may stop participating before submitting your completed ratings.

## Human Grading Instructions

You will grade **54 model responses**. For each row:

1. Read the system instruction and task prompt.
2. Read the response to evaluate.
3. Read the reference answer (an example of a score-5 response).
4. Read the score rubric carefully.
5. Enter a single integer score (**1–5**) in the score column, based strictly on the rubric—not on your general impression of response quality.
6. Optionally, note your reasoning in the comments column.

## AI Assistance Policy

Do **NOT** use any AI/LLM tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.) to help decide scores. You may look up unfamiliar words or general facts if needed to understand a task, but the scoring judgment itself must be entirely your own.

## Notes

- Item order is randomized and differs between raters. This is intentional.
- Some system instructions may contain unusual wording, typos, or phrasing. Grade the response on its merits per the rubric regardless of how the instruction is phrased.
- Please complete all rows in one or a few sittings if possible, to keep your grading standards consistent across items.
